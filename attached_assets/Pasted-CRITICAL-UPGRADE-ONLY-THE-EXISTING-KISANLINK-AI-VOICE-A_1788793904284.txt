5. CONVERSATIONAL MEMORY
==================================================

The assistant must maintain conversation context during the current session.

Example:

Farmer:
"My farm is in Nashik."

Assistant:
"Okay."

Farmer:
"I grow onions."

Assistant:
"Understood."

Farmer:
"When should I sell them?"

The assistant should understand that "them" refers to onions.

Maintain a conversation history array and send the relevant conversation history to the LLM.

Do not reset the context after every message.

Add reasonable limits so the conversation history does not grow infinitely.
