7. CROP QUALITY CHECK — DO NOT JUDGE FROM ONE IMAGE
====================================================

This is another VERY IMPORTANT requirement.

The current quality-check system appears to depend too heavily on one uploaded image.

CHANGE THE WORKFLOW.

The AI must NOT decide crop/produce quality based on a single image.

When the farmer starts a Quality Check, create a structured data-collection process.

FIRST ASK FOR THE REQUIRED INFORMATION.

Collect:

1. Crop/produce type
2. Variety if known
3. Harvest date
4. Quantity
5. Storage condition
6. Where it has been stored
7. Handling information
8. Visible problems/damage
9. Location/district
10. Any other relevant crop-specific details

ASK:
"When did you harvest this crop/produce?"

Use the harvest date to calculate:

Days since harvest.

Example:

Harvest date = 5 September
Current date = 8 September

Days since harvest = 3 days

Do NOT invent the harvest date.
